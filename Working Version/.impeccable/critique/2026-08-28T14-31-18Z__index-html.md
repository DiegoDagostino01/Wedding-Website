# Critique — Index.html (Diego & Bethany wedding site)

## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Start here: does this look AI-generated? No.** The page reads bespoke on every axis: eight hand-built SVG flower symbols with radial-gradient volume shading, a scroll-drawn vine with per-flower bloom windows, couple-specific copy (Covid-era coursework, the Geiranger proposal), real venues with real drive times, and a working Google Apps Script RSVP backend. A visitor asks "who made this?", not "which AI made this?".

**LLM assessment**: Not slop. One Script Moment (Great Vibes only on the names and the RSVP sign-off) is honored; the palette is committed rather than timid; the travel copy answers real guest questions rather than filling layout. Residual tells are small: the 01–04 ghost numerals on journey cards are an editorial-scaffold habit, and em-dash cadence in the prose is heavy (20 in body copy).

**Deterministic scan** (`detect.mjs --json Index.html`, exit 2 — 3 findings):
- `single-font` (warning, "only font used is great vibes") — **false positive**. The page loads three families (Great Vibes, Noto Serif Display, Montserrat); the detector appears to have read only the hero script face.
- `em-dash-overuse` (warning, 20 em-dashes in body copy) — **legitimate but mild**. Wedding prose tolerates dashes, but several could become commas, colons, or periods.
- `numbered-section-markers` (advisory, sequence 01–04) — **legitimate**. The giant translucent numerals on journey stage cards are a recognizable editorial pattern; the botanical pin motif already on the cards could carry the sequence instead.

**Visual overlays**: not available this run — no browser automation tool is exposed in this session, so no injection and no user-visible overlay. Fallback signal: full source read (Index.html, styles.css, scripts.js) plus the CLI scan above.

## Overall Impression

A wedding site with a genuine point of view and unusual engineering care. The botanical system is the identity, the guest logistics are the substance, and the accessibility floor is far above what static sites usually ship. The risks all live at the edges: the RSVP form's failure modes (the one moment that matters most), multi-megabyte PNG delivery, and the map section's two competing "brains". Fix the edges and this is excellent.

## What's Working

1. **The botanical illustration system is hand-made, not template.** Eight bespoke SVG flower symbols (rose, daisy, craspedia, delphinium, scabiosa, tulip, leaf, sprig) with core-shadow and sheen gradients, placed along a scroll-drawn vine with per-flower bloom windows via CSS custom properties. This is the page's signature and it cannot be mistaken for stock.
2. **Guest-first content design is exceptional.** Route planner with named data sources and a graceful estimate fallback, last-trains warning, "book the taxi" note, copy-postcode, lift-share via the RSVP message box, honest "confirming soon" labels. It anticipates the questions guests actually ask, in the order they ask them.
3. **The accessibility floor is far above typical static sites.** Progressive-enhancement reveal gating (content visible unless JS + IO + motion all agree), `inert` on the collapsed party details, a real combobox pattern for the guest picker, aria-live status regions, focus-visible styles everywhere, 44px coarse-pointer floors, reduced-motion honored in both CSS and JS.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] RSVP form breaks when the guest sheet can't load — and short queries get silence.**
Why it matters: this is the conversion moment of the entire site. If the Apps Script GET fails (CORS blip, network, script quota), the placeholder switches to "Your full name" but validation still requires a selected list guest (`nameOk = !!selectedGuest`), so every submission is rejected — the promised free-text fallback is a dead end. Separately, below 4 typed characters the dropdown stays hidden with no "keep typing…" feedback, and names that never reach 4 letters can never match.
Fix: when `GUEST_LIST` is empty after load, switch validation to accept the typed name; show an inline hint under the 4-char threshold; special-case short names.
Suggested command: `impeccable harden`

**[P1] Multi-megabyte page weight in the wrong formats.**
Why it matters: the hero is a 1024×1024 PNG preloaded at `fetchpriority="high"`; the dress guide is a 1536×1024 PNG; gallery and watercolour photos are PNGs; Google Fonts load three families with ~15 variants; Leaflet (~145KB) initializes even for guests who never scroll near the map. On a phone over 4G at a rural venue (the site itself warns signal is patchy) this is seconds of blank hero.
Fix: convert photographic/raster art to WebP/AVIF (JPEG fallback), subset fonts to the weights actually used, lazy-init Leaflet when `#storymap` approaches the viewport.
Suggested command: `impeccable optimize`

**[P1] Journey-map caption has two writers.**
Why it matters: after Leaflet initializes, `#storymap.leaflet-ready .journey-map-camera` is hidden — but the SVG scroll system keeps running, and both it (`stageLabels`) and Leaflet's card MutationObserver (`name · sub`) write to `#journeyMapCaption` on every scroll. The caption visibly flickers between two label formats.
Fix: when `.leaflet-ready` is set, unbind the SVG system's scroll handler (or give Leaflet's module sole ownership of the caption).
Suggested command: `impeccable harden`

**[P2] "Happening now" tag fails contrast.**
Why it matters: white 0.66rem uppercase text on `--warm-deep` #D98368 is ≈2.8:1 — well below the 4.5:1 AA floor. It is the day-of status cue, exactly when guests squint at phones in sunlight.
Fix: darken the tag background toward ink or #C25B6E, or flip to ink text on the blush tint.
Suggested command: `impeccable polish`

**[P2] styles.css structural rot.**
Why it matters: a body typography block is orphaned outside any selector (font-kerning, -webkit-font-smoothing, text-rendering are dead); a stray closing brace follows the `#rsvp` pointer-fine media query; the footer uses `var(--soft-ink)` which is undefined (falls back to full ink); legacy warm token names (--peach/--gold/--terracotta) hold cool blue values. Each is small; together they make every future edit riskier.
Fix: repair the orphaned block and stray brace, define or replace --soft-ink, and rename tokens to their role-based names as DESIGN.md already intends.
Suggested command: `impeccable polish`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer)**: types "Bo", sees no dropdown and no feedback (4-char threshold, no hint). If the guest list fails to load, the form promises free text then rejects every submission. Will email the couple confused, or not at all.

**Sam (accessibility-dependent)**: strong baseline overall. Gaps: mobile nav toggle lacks `aria-expanded`; the drawer has no Escape close or focus trap; lightbox focus is not returned to the invoking figure; the map caption and journey labels render at 10px; the Leaflet swap silently replaces the SVG map's title/description for screen readers (the stage cards remain the accessible path, but nothing labels that).

**Casey (distracted mobile user)**: multi-megabyte first paint over a connection the site itself calls patchy; petals + vine + Leaflet camera lerp all animate on scroll on low-end GPUs. Tap targets and state (disabled button, spinners) are exemplary.

**Riley (stress tester)**: `mode: 'no-cors'` makes every resolved fetch read as success — an Apps Script 500 still shows "your reply is in". No client-side deadline guard after 28 Feb 2027. OSRM demo server has no SLA (graceful fallback exists). Double-submit is guarded; the countdown shows 00s forever after the day while the schedule's "What a day that was" handles it gracefully.

## Minor Observations

- `footer .foot-contact` uses undefined `--soft-ink`.
- 12-hour "1:00 PM" schedule vs 24-hour "23:09" train times in the same section.
- No `og:`/`twitter:` meta — the link will share bare in group chats; no `theme-color` either.
- Countdown after the wedding day shows 00s instead of a graceful state.
- Five parallel reveal implementations (.reveal, [data-tr], [data-dr], [data-hm], [data-faq], [data-rv]) — one observer factory would do.
- Stale comments in scripts.js mislabel sections ("// Stay sort" above the hero parallax).
- Dark-map token set (--map-dark, red #e7192c pins) documented in DESIGN.md front-matter belongs to the previous design world; the light illustrated map is the current one.
- DESIGN.md's overview paragraph ("Montserrat throughout… Dancing Script") contradicts its own token block (Great Vibes + Noto Serif Display) — worth one editing pass so the document matches the shipped identity.

## Questions to Consider

- What if the RSVP flow degraded gracefully by design — a typed-name path that always works when the sheet cannot be reached?
- What if the journey map had one brain instead of two — the illustrated SVG for low-motion contexts, Leaflet as the only owner of the caption?
- What would this page weigh if every photographic asset were WebP and the fonts were subset to the six weights actually used?
